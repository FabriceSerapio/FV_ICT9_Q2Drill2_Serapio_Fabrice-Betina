A DIAMOND LEGACY OF LEARNING


"At Operation Brotherhood Montessori Center, each child is nurtured in the holistic method of Dr. Maria Montessori."


Welcome to the O.B. Montessori Center in the Philippines!


We are a progressive pre-k to 12 co-educational school, offering a viable alternative to international schools. At OBMC, we blend tradition and innovation to prepare a child for life.


Our mission is to transform our students into self-developing and productive individuals who can succeed, direct, and influence the future of an ever-changing world.


Explore our programs and discover the vibrant community that makes OBMC a unique place for a child to grow and develop.



Campuses


In a safe and nurturing environment, our campuses offer accessible, high-quality education for learning, creativity, and development.


Come and visit our facilities to see and experience what each campus can offer your child and family.



Sta. Ana

Our OBMC Sta. Ana Campus is located in the heritage district of Manila. It is known for its strong academic programs, community involvement, and love for culture and heritage.




Angeles

Our OBMC Angeles Campus is located in the culturally rich and vibrant province of Pampanga. Both academically and personally, it provides a nurturing and dynamic Montessori environment for students to thrive.




Greenhills

Our Greenhills Campus in San Juan City offers a creative and fun environment to spark the imagination, love of learning, and service to the community of the students.




Las Piñas

Our Las Piñas Campus pays homage to the area's cultural history, the birthplace of the Sarao jeepney, and the unique 19th Bamboo Organ. Through its welcoming atmosphere, it encourages the development and learning of students.




Fairview

Our Fairview Campus located in Quezon City delivers accessible quality montessori education to its community of young families.
